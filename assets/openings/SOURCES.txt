Offline chess opening catalogue

Source: Lichess chess-openings
https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings
License: CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The TSV catalogue supplies opening names, ECO codes, and representative move
sequences. It does not contain master-game win/draw statistics. Those are
calculated locally from the PGN files in data/master_pgn.
